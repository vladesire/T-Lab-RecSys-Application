# Research-state review port v1

The reviewer receives only view.json, its selected hypothesis/decision records and referenced evidence summaries. No infrastructure implementation source is necessary.

The joined view distinguishes canonical intent/judgment from derived execution/evidence. It lists planned/staged/failed/blocked/completed work, superseded decisions, uncertainty, source IDs and exclusions. A view's omission of another branch is not evidence that branch never existed.

Return: observations; inference; unsupported tempting conclusions; unresolved checks; at most three next actions with information value, cost, controls and stop conditions; proposed decision/journal text with exact evidence references. Identify late exploratory work and holdout restrictions. Model text does not become canonical until the researcher records a decision; no execution or source editing is requested by a review packet alone.

Record exact known author/model/settings; unknown metadata remains null. A new hypothesis written after inspecting development results must retain that chronology. Do not infer historical preregistration from a clean current configuration.
